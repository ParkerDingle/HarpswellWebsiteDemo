Signature Urns

We have been hand-crafting urns for 35 years, and have a number of sizes and styles of urns available. We personalize each urn to fit the needs of the family, and are able to create custom urns if one of our standard sizes doesn't meet your needs.

We can add pictures to the urns, either by sublimation or, in many cases, by inscribing the image directly onto the urn.

Please contact us for more information about the urn sizes and the customization and personalization options that are available.
