Coasters & Paperweights

Coasters and paperweights make great gifts for outgoing board of directors, speakers, or volunteers. Each gift can be personalized with a message and/or logo.

Paperweights are available in natural cleft or honed surface; the honed finish may be accented with a wood inlay. Coasters are available in natural cleft, with your choice of shape (round or square) and color (black or green).

Additional options available by special order. Please contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
