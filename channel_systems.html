Channel Systems

Harpswell House knows how important your donors are to your organization. We also know how important your staff or your members are to your goals. Our channel system allows you maximum flexibility to add, remove, or rearrange pieces for life-time giving programs, annual recognition, directory systems, or any other on-going projects that you might want to institute for your organization, so they can see that you value them, too.

The system will be designed to accommodate your needs; from single columns to a multi-column unit. We have cherry, oak, and mahogany in stock, but your channel system can be made from most hardwoods.

Nameplates can be made from black, green or red slate and can be made in multiple heights. Choose between two lengths: 6" or 11".

We also offer a smaller, multi-column system. The standard plate is 1"x6" and can be made up to 5 columns wide and any height you choose.

A channel system can also work in combination with a plaque. Elements of your program that don't change can be inscribed on a plaque; or position a description of the program next to the channels to encourage others to join. Perhaps you'd like to recognize the highest level donors differently than the others to emphasize their contributions. Combining a plaque with your channels may be the solution that you've been looking for.

We'd love to talk to you about what would work best for you. Contact us with your requirements and let us design a system that will meet your needs and complement your facility.
