Harpswell, Maine · Craftsmen in Stone for Fifty Years

Online Store

Harpswell House has been crafting unique and distinctive products from domestic natural slate since 1960. Each item is carefully hand crafted at our Maine workshop, and since we work with a natural material no two pieces are exactly alike.

Our gifts are designed to be of heirloom quality and will provide years of enjoyment and service. Most pieces can be personalized with a name, message, or logo — unique gifts for holidays, anniversaries, weddings, birthdays, or corporate giving.
