Three Stones, One Shop

Pennsylvania Black

Grey-black slate from Eastern Pennsylvania. Our workhorse for donor walls and building signage — quiet, formal, and legible for generations.

Vermont Green

Soft sea-green with natural veining. A favorite for coasters, bookends and garden memorials, often paired with teak inlay.

Vermont Red

Rare oxblood red, formed by iron-rich mud in prehistoric lake beds. Used as an accent to marry two colors on a single plaque.
