Awards & Plaques

Show your donors how much you appreciate their support, or recognize your top performers by giving them an heirloom quality gift that will never go out of style. An award made from 300 million year old slate will certainly be an attention getter. Our plaques have been used for many kinds of awards, from employee recognition programs, committee retirements, and safety programs to individual plaques to recognize large achievements.

We can help make your next award special. The natural cleft surface of slate generally has ridges, swirls and bumps. For a more upscale feel, the honed surface has a luxurious satiny smooth finish. Incorporating a wood inlay into the honed slate elevates the design beyond the ordinary. For the ultimate award, choose a plaque composed of multiple colors and a wood accent; your recipient will know just how important they are to you.

To have an award designed for your company or group, please contact us. We will work closely with you to design an award to meet your specific requirements.
