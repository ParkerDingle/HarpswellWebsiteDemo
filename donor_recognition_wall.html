Donor Recognition Walls

A natural slate wall system is distinctive and elegant; adding wood accents brings the trim features of the building right onto the plaque, allowing the wall system to become an integral part of the building's design.

Each donor wall is a unique combination of element and design and is carefully crafted to meet your specific needs. Choose black slate from Pennsylvania, green or red slate from Vermont, or a combination of colors to create a look that will enhance your facility as well as appropriately honor your donors.

We take time to listen to your needs and goals, and work with you to create a permanent display that your organization and donors will be proud of. We look forward to helping you achieve the unique look that will express your gratitude to everyone who has helped make your project a reality. Please contact us for more information or to discuss your requirements with our staff.
