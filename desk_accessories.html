Desk Accessories & Gifts

We have a great selection of gifts that can be used to recognize the important people in your organization – anyone from the retiring chairman of the board to the special volunteer you can’t do without. Each item can be personalized with a message and/or logo to allow you to convey to the recipient how much you appreciate them and what a difference they make in your organization.

Bookends are one of our most popular products. Our natural cleft slate pen-sets or time & weather stations look great on an executive desk. A Slate box is an impressive way to let someone know they are important. The boxes are made from natural cleft slate, and include a removable cover and a felted bottom. The picture frame holds a picture in a clear acrylic sleeve mounted on the slate.

Our gifts are available individually or in quantity. Please contact us to have a gift designed to meet your specific requirements.
