Naming Opportunities

Naming plaques are very versatile, and can be used inside or out to identify the important people, places and things within an organization.

Choose a single color of slate; marry two colors; add a wood accent; the combinations and possibilities abound. We specialize in individual pieces and use time-tested hand-craftsmanship to allow you to customize a plaque for each need, area or building.

Our designers are skilled at working with a client to ensure their recognition project reflects their organization's vision. Contact us for more information. We'd love to help with your next project.
